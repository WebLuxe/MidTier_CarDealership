A family forecourt on Royal Road since 2011

WebLuxe Cars started with four cars and a tin roof. Today we keep around forty on the forecourt, and we still buy every one of them ourselves.

We would rather lose a sale than hide a fault. Each car gets a workshop inspection before it goes on sale, and you can bring your own mechanic to look at it.

See all 15 cars

What you get with every car

  • Inspected before it is listed

    Engine, gearbox, brakes, suspension and electrics are checked in our workshop, and you get the written report.

  • The price on the windscreen is the price

    No hidden fees at the desk. Registration transfer is included.

  • Leasing arranged on the spot

    We work with the main leasing companies, and most answers come back the same day.

  • Your old car taken in part exchange

    Bring it along. We value it while you test drive, and take it off the price.

Four checks before a car goes on sale

  1. Engine and gearbox

    Compression, leaks, mounts and a road test through every gear.

  2. Body and paint

    Panel gaps and paint depth measured, so repaired accident damage has nowhere to hide.

  3. Mileage and papers

    Service book, import papers and NTA records compared with the odometer.

  4. Tyres and brakes

    At least 4 mm of tread and half the pad life left, or we replace them.
