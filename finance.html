Common questions

What do I need to apply?

Your ID card, a proof of address less than three months old, and your last three payslips or bank statements. Self-employed buyers bring their business registration as well.

How long does approval take?

Most applications are answered the same day or the next working day. We send the papers to our leasing partners for you.

Can I pay it off early?

Yes. The leasing company gives you a settlement figure at any time. Ask us and we will request it for you.

Can my old car be the deposit?

Yes. We value your car and its value counts as your deposit, in full or in part.
